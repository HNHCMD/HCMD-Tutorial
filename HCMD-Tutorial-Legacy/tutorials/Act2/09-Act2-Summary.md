# **09 — Summary**

Act II revealed the architecture behind the HCMD loop.  
Act I showed the performance; Act II explained why the performance works.

The core insight is simple:

> **Co‑intelligence is only stable when meaning and execution are separated.**

The Two‑Copilot Model expresses this separation through two engines:

- a **meaning‑side engine** that interprets and refines intent  
- a **structure‑side engine** that derives structure and generates implementation  

These engines meet at a single boundary artifact:

- the **STS** — deterministic, complete, implementation‑free  

Above the boundary, ambiguity is allowed and refinement is required.  
Below the boundary, ambiguity is forbidden and determinism is enforced.

This separation produces:

- stable refinement  
- clean structure  
- reproducible implementation  
- predictable regeneration  

When the engines mix roles, the loop collapses.  
When the boundary is preserved, the loop becomes a discipline.

Act II provides the architectural understanding needed to see HCMD not as a workflow, but as a system — one that can be reproduced, extended, and applied across domains.

Act III will reveal the deeper principle that makes this architecture necessary.
