# 00 — How to Read Act II

Act II explains the architecture behind the HCMD loop.  
Where Act 1 is a performance, Act 2 is an analysis: it reveals the two engines,
the layer boundaries, and the structural logic that makes HCMD reproducible.

This Act is written under the **HCMD Hut**, the stance used for producing HCMD
artifacts. The HCMD Hut and its blueprint are located in:
    huts/shop/

You do not need to understand huts to read Act 2.  
They define the stance, not the content.

---

## How to Approach This Act

Read Act 2 as a conceptual map.

It does not show the loop in motion.  
It explains **why** the loop works, **how** the engines divide responsibility,
and **what** must remain stable for the discipline to function.

Do not treat Act II as a reference manual.  
It is a structural explanation, not a catalog of rules.

---

## What Act 2 Provides

Act 2 gives you:

- the reason two Copilots exist  
- the separation between meaning and structure  
- the boundary that prevents layer collapse  
- the mapping between engines and HCMD layers  
- the determinism requirements  
- the failure modes that appear when boundaries are violated  
- the general pattern that all HCMD work follows  

This Act is the **architectural foundation** of the discipline.

---

## How to Read It

Read Act II slowly.  
Each chapter builds on the previous one.

Do not skip ahead.  
Do not attempt to “apply” the ideas yet.  
Your goal is to understand the **shape** of the discipline.

Act II prepares you for Act 3, where the architecture becomes explicit.
