# 02 — The Meaning‑Side Engine (Copilot App)

The meaning‑side engine is responsible for everything that happens **before** the STS becomes deterministic.  
It is the part of the system that works with the human to clarify intent, resolve ambiguity, and stabilize meaning.

This engine does not generate code.  
It does not create structure.  
It does not enforce conventions.

Its role is semantic:

- interpret the human goal  
- surface ambiguities  
- refine intent through dialogue  
- converge on a complete, implementation‑free STS  

The meaning‑side engine is where the human and the assistant think together.  
It is the only place where ambiguity is allowed, because ambiguity is the raw material of meaning.

## What the meaning‑side engine produces

- **clarified intent**  
- **semantic interpretation**  
- **refined tasks**  
- **the STS** — the first explicit, deterministic artifact  

The STS is the output of the meaning‑side engine.  
It is the hinge of the HCMD loop.

## Why this engine must be separate

If the meaning‑side engine tries to generate code, it collapses the layers.  
If it tries to infer structure, it contaminates the STS.  
If it mixes meaning with implementation, it breaks determinism.

The meaning‑side engine must remain above the boundary:

- ambiguity allowed  
- refinement required  
- no structure  
- no code  

Its strength is interpretation, not execution.

## Skills (Huts) inside the Meaning‑Side Engine

- Skills are the internal containers of meaning.
- They form during STS refinement.
- They are not code modules.
- They are reused across tasks and projects.

This is the correct conceptual home.

## Why Copilot App fits this role

Copilot App is optimized for:

- conversation  
- semantic reasoning  
- iterative refinement  
- resolving ambiguity  
- stabilizing meaning  

It excels when the input is human‑level and open‑ended.  
This is exactly what the meaning layer requires.

The meaning‑side engine works because it receives **human intent**.  
It fails when it receives **implementation details** too early.

This separation is the foundation of the HCMD loop.
