# **Act 4 — Stability Over Time**

Act 4 explains why HCMD remains stable across languages, tools, and years.  
It shows how PSC preserves structure, how deterministic formats prevent drift, and how the HCMD loop avoids unstable feedback cycles.

This Act is written under the **HCMD Hut**, the stance used for producing HCMD artifacts.  
The HCMD Hut and its blueprint are located in `huts/shop/`.

You do not need to understand huts to read Act 4.  
They define the stance, not the content.

## **What Act 4 Demonstrates**

Act 4 presents the temporal stability model of HCMD:

1. why PSC must be saved  
2. why deterministic formats matter  
3. why XML is the correct medium for PSC  
4. how PSC stabilizes the pipeline  
5. why HCMD has no unstable looping  
6. how cross‑language regeneration works  
7. how long‑term reproducibility is preserved  
8. how HCMD differs from agentic systems  

Act 4 is about **time** — how HCMD stays correct as everything else changes.

Read it slowly.
