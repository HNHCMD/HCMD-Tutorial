# **00‑First‑Loop — We Start From Something Very Small**

## **0.1  Before Anything Else, We Do Something Tiny**

Most tutorials begin with installation steps, system requirements, or a long explanation of the theory.

This one does not.

We begin with something much smaller.  
Almost embarrassingly small.

We begin by making a logo.

Not because logos are important.  
Not because this one is special.  
But simply because it is the smallest possible task we can start with.

That is enough for now.

---

## **0.2  The Logo: A Simple Spiral**

The logo we create here is:

- simple  
- monochrome  
- geometric  
- clean lines  
- no text  

Just a spiral.

Nothing more.

If you have ever played with image‑generation tools, you already know how this goes:

You ask for a simple spiral.  
It gives you something that is almost a spiral.  
You try again.  
It becomes too thick.  
You try again.  
It becomes too thin.  
You try again.  
It becomes a seashell, or a galaxy, or a cinnamon roll.

You adjust the prompt.  
You simplify the prompt.  
You over‑explain.  
You under‑explain.

Eventually, you get something close enough.

This is normal.

And strangely, it is also fun.

---

## **0.3  “Why Are We Doing This?”**

At this point, you may be thinking:

> “Why are we starting a tutorial with a spiral logo?  
> Isn’t this trivial?  
> Isn’t this far below what we usually do with image tools?”  

Yes.  
Exactly.

Graphic design is already a crowded field.  
People generate far more impressive images every day — dragons, movie posters, photorealistic landscapes, impossible architecture.

And here we are, making a spiral.

This mismatch is intentional.

---

## **0.4  Your First Task**

Generate a simple spiral logo:

> **“Generate a simple, monochrome spiral logo that symbolizes an iterative, evolving process. Minimal, geometric, clean lines, no text.”**

Save whatever you get.  
It does not need to be perfect.  
It only needs to exist.

That is enough for the first loop.

---

## **0.5  A Small Note Before You Go**

If you are feeling a slight  
**“Why start with this?”**  
or  
**“This is too trivial.”**

— that feeling is entirely intentional.

Welcome to the first loop.
