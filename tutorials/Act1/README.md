# Act 1 — First Contact with the HCMD Loop

Act 1 is a performance of the HCMD loop.  
It shows how meaning becomes tasks, how tasks become structure, and how structure becomes implementation.

This Act 1s written under the **HCMD Hut**, the stance used for producing HCMD artifacts.  
The HCMD Hut and its blueprint are located in: huts/shop/

You do not need to understand huts to read Act 1.  
They define the stance, not the content.

---

## What Act 1 Demonstrates

Act 1 presents the HCMD loop in motion:

1. the initial human goal  
2. semantic interpretation  
3. emergence and refinement of the STS  
4. deterministic implementation in C#  
5. semantic portability in Java  
6. a reconstructed behind‑the‑scenes log  
7. a conceptual explanation of the HCMD layers  

Act 1 is not an exercise.  
It is the first contact with the discipline.

Read it straight through.  
Do not attempt to reproduce the steps.  
Do not optimize.  
Simply watch the loop work.
