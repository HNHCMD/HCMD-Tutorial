# 0. How to Read This Tutorial

This tutorial is a **performance**, not a set of examples.

Act 1 shows the HCMD loop in motion:

- a human goal  
- semantic interpretation  
- emergence of the STS  
- deterministic implementation  
- language‑independent reproducibility  

You are not expected to reproduce the steps yet.  
You are expected to **watch the loop work**.

---

## Your Role as the Reader

Read Act 1 with a **question mark**, not a checklist.

When something feels unclear, incomplete, or structurally odd, that reaction is intentional.  
HCMD is a discipline built on refinement.  
Your question marks are part of the learning loop.

Do not try to “follow along” with your own tools.  
Do not attempt to replicate the commands or code.  
Act 1 is not an exercise — it is a demonstration.

---

## Why This Matters

HCMD is not a feature of Copilot.  
It is a way of working with Copilot.

To understand the discipline, you must first see:

- how meaning becomes tasks  
- how tasks become structure  
- how structure becomes implementation  

Only after watching the loop can you begin to perform it.

---

## A Note on Huts

This tutorial is written under the **HCMD Hut**, the stance used for producing HCMD artifacts.  
A hut is a *mode of thinking*, not part of the HCMD loop itself.

You do not need to understand huts to read Act 1.  
They simply define the **stance** from which the material is written.

The official huts and their definitions live in:

```
huts/shop/
```

They are part of the discipline’s supporting architecture, not part of the tutorial content.
