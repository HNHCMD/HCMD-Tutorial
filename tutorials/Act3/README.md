# Act 3 — The Principle Behind the Two‑Engine Architecture

Act 3 explains the principle that makes the two‑engine architecture possible.
Where Act 1 shows the loop in motion, and Act 2 explains the architecture,
Act 3 reveals the cognitive separation that underlies the entire discipline.

This Act is written under the **HCMD Hut**, the stance used for producing HCMD
artifacts. The HCMD Hut and its blueprint are located in: huts/shop/

You do not need to understand huts to read Act 3.
They define the stance, not the content.

---

## Purpose of Act 3

Act 3 introduces the **Cognitive Separation Principle**:

- Meaning and Execution must remain cognitively separate.
- The two engines exist to enforce this separation.
- The HCMD loop is only stable when the separation is preserved.
- All failure modes in Act 2 trace back to violations of this principle.

Act 3 is not about mechanics.
It is about the **why** behind the architecture.

## How to Read This Act

Read Act 3 as a philosophical argument with structural consequences.
It is not a tutorial, not a demonstration, and not an implementation.

It is the **principle** that makes the architecture in Act 2 coherent and the
performance in Act 1 possible.

Act 3 prepares you for Act 4, where the implications of the principle are
applied to real workflows.
