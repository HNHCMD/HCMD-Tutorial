# 00 — How to Read Act 3

Act 3 presents the principle that makes the two‑engine architecture possible.
Where Act 1 shows the loop in motion, and Act 2 explains the architecture,
Act 3 reveals the cognitive separation that underlies the entire discipline.

This Act is written under the **HCMD Hut**, the stance used for producing HCMD
artifacts. The HCMD Hut and its blueprint are located in:
    huts/shop/

You do not need to understand huts to read Act 3.
They define the stance, not the content.

---

## How to Approach This Act

Read Act 3 as a conceptual argument.

It does not describe mechanics.  
It does not refine an STS.  
It does not show implementation.

Instead, it explains **why** the two engines must exist and **why** the HCMD
loop is only stable when meaning and structure remain cognitively separate.

---

## What Act 3 Provides

Act 3 gives you:

- the principle of separation between meaning and execution  
- the cognitive reason one engine cannot do both  
- the structural consequences of violating the separation  
- the generalization of the principle beyond Copilot  
- the implications for reproducibility, determinism, and co‑intelligence  

Act 3 is the conceptual anchor of the discipline.

---

## How to Read It

Read Act 3 slowly.  
Each chapter builds on the previous one.

Do not treat it as a tutorial.  
Do not attempt to “apply” the ideas yet.

Your goal is to understand the **principle** that makes the architecture in
Act 2 coherent and the performance in Act 1 possible.

Act 3 prepares you for Act 4, where the implications of the principle are
applied to real workflows.
